# Turbo Tempo Racing — Game Plan v1

**Style:** late-90s arcade racer, behind-the-car 3D chase view, landscape iPhone. Original art and names only. Paid app, no ads, no IAP, no data collected. Credit: Backline Vintage.

## Core loop
Pick a driver and car, race through cups, place high to unlock the next cup, earn new cars, paint jobs, and trophies. No story.

## Version 1 mechanics (keep it simple)
1. **Steering + drift** with touch controls.
2. **Instrument move:** each car has one built-in move, charged by clean driving (no random item boxes, no weapons).
3. **Note gates:** gates show notes; driving through the ones that match the melody gives a speed boost.
4. **Style score:** rank by finish place plus style (clean lines, on-melody gates), like a band competition score.

## Later updates
- Rhythm drafting (tap on the beat behind a car to slingshot past)
- Tempo shifts (the music's tempo changes the track: drawbridges, ramps, traffic)
- Point-to-point parade routes with branching shortcuts
- Time trial, mirror mode, harder speed class

## Cups (4 tracks each)
- **Brass Cup** — Brass City: trumpet skyscrapers, piano-key crosswalks. Teaches steering + instrument moves.
- **Woodwind Cup** — Woodwind Forest: reed grass, flute-pipe tunnels. Introduces note gates.
- **Percussion Cup** — Percussion Canyon: drum-head bounce pads, rolling timpani. (Rhythm drafting here once added.)
- **Grand Finale Cup** — Concert Hall and parade routes. Everything combined.
Prize for clearing all: the Diamond Baton trophy.

## Cars and drivers (gender-neutral band students)
Each car has tradeoffs tied to its instrument, shown as Speed / Toughness / Handling / Boost bars.

| Car | Pro | Con | Instrument move |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tuba Truck | Toughest; bumps barely slow it | Sluggish to speed up and turn | Bass Blast pushes nearby cars aside |
| Piccolo Racer | Fastest top speed | Fragile; bumps cost big speed | High Trill short burst |
| Trombone Coupe | Balanced all-rounder | Average top speed | Slide Dash corner-cutting lunge |
| Snare Kart | Best handling, quick drifts | Light; gets pushed around | Drum Roll grip boost through turns |
| French Horn Cruiser | Fills move meter fastest | Twitchy steering | Horn Call clears the lane ahead |
| Sax Roadster | Biggest boost from note gates | Slow off the starting line | Smooth Riff long gentle boost |

## Hazards
Music stands, rolling timpani, metronome swing gates, sheet-music puddles.

## Music
Each track has its own band tune. Moving up in place adds band sections, so first place sounds like the full band.
